# P4 red team — the refutation record

Three independent refuters were pointed at every new opinion constant this phase shipped — the
pool-skill dial's domain, its interior blend, its plays-better coefficient and its admission; the
EV MIX band's `k` and every figure published beside it; the two quantities the plan declared
introduce *no* new constant (`evStake`, `evPrimary`); and the three byte ceremonies — with one
instruction: **falsify it**. Perturb the number, delete the flag, replace the anchor's prose with a
lie, and report what the repository does about it. Twenty tally keys drew **sixty memos**. This file
is the record, committed with the phase.

**Why it is a file and not a paragraph.** The house rule is *constants need anchors*: a number
either stands on a measurement, or it ships flagged, gated and badged with the reason it cannot.
That rule is only worth what the audit behind it is worth, and an audit that leaves no trace is a
claim rather than a check. The memos below are reproduced **verbatim**, including the places where
refuters disagree with each other, the places where a memo is wrong about the repository, and every
sentence critical of work this phase had just finished. Nothing was trimmed to look better. Two
memos contradict each other outright about whether the page's slider reads `SKILL.min` (it does —
`wireVP` copies it, `src/shell.html`'s static attribute is overwritten), and both are printed as
written, because a red team that is quietly corrected before publication is a red team nobody can
audit.

## What changed because of it

Four repairs, one gate strengthening, and a set of recorded findings that changed no code. Every
repair was **armed** before it was believed: the perturbation that shipped green in a refuter's
memo was re-run against the new clause and made to fail.

### 1. The dial's domain — the one anchor that was refuted outright

**What was refuted, precisely.** `constants.skill` shipped `min: 0, ref: 0, max: 1` under the claim
that they are *anchored by construction* — that `poolVpip`'s two early returns (`s === ref` returns
`v` itself, `s === max` returns `vFloor` itself) are what makes the endpoints exact. Three refuters
of three refuted it, and not academically:

* `min = -1` ships **60/60 gates, 632 tests and 2/2 variants current**, and it opens the *loosen*
  half of the axis — `poolVpip(55, -1) = 85` — which is the plays-better side V3-PLAN §3.6's Grade C
  does not build. `wireVP` copies `SKILL.min` straight onto the page's slider (`sk.min = SKILL.min`),
  so this is a setting a reader can select, not a theoretical one.
* `max = 2` ships green: half the dial then sits past the measured floor.
* `ref = 0.05` ships green, putting a *loosening step* between two adjacent stops of the page's own
  0.01 slider and leaving the lobby unreachable.
* the construction argument itself is vacuous: **both early returns are removable with everything
  green.** `v + 0*(f - v)` is `v` for every finite `v`, and `v + 1*(25 - v)` is exactly `25` for
  every double in [25, 90] — one refuter brute-forced 3,000,000 draws.
* the only failures the refuters *could* provoke (`ref = 0.1`, `max = 0.9`) came from colliding with
  I37(b)'s hardcoded probe list `{0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9}` — "a coincidence of sampling, not a
  bound".

**Disposition — no replacement anchor was invented.** V3-PLAN §6's rule when an anchor is refuted is
the flagged idiom, and that is what the triple now ships as:

| leg | before | now |
|---|---|---|
| named in `constants` | the fields, with the anchor claim in a comment | a third record in `constants.skill.flag`, naming `min`/`ref`/`max` and saying what P4 did to their anchor — paid for by trimming the flag, not by raising D6's 1K sub-budget |
| labelled in the Method view | rendered as ordinary fields | `UNANCHORED['skill.min']`, `['skill.ref']`, `['skill.max']` — the `estimate` badge, beside `blend` and `playsBetter` |
| bounded by a gate | nothing — no gate and no test asserted any of the three | **I38(g)**, below |

**I38(g), and why it is a bound rather than a re-anchoring.** The clause asserts that the domain is
**forced** by two things that are already anchored — the published blend and the measured floor —
and then sweeps the dial at the page's own slider step:

1. the published blend returns the pool itself **only** at `ref` and the floor **only** at `max`
   (`v + s*(f - v) = v ⟺ s = 0`, `= f ⟺ s = 1`), so those two numbers are pinned by the blend and
   the floor between them rather than by a literal in the gate;
2. `poolVpip(v, min)` is the lobby itself, so a lower bound below the lobby fails on sight;
3. over **909 settings** at the page's own step (`0.01`, read back out of `src/shell.html` together
   with its `SKILL.min`/`SKILL.max` wiring), the dial never moves a pool **up**, never reaches the
   floor **before** `max`, and is monotone throughout.

Armed: `min = -1`, `max = 2`, `max = 0.9`, `ref = 0.05`, `ref = 0.5` and `min = 0.5` are each red
now, and dropping the domain from the flag is red at I38(f). It is a **bound, not an anchor** —
`max = 1` is a unit convention normalised against a blend that is itself §6's unanchorable row —
which is exactly why the badge stays on.

### 2. The interior blend — the legs held, the gate did not bound the curve

All three legs §6 requires are present and all three fire when attacked (a desynchronised spelling
fails I37(b) character-for-character; a stubbed flag and a missing `UNANCHORED` entry fail I38(f)).
What three refuters demonstrated, independently and with three different curves, is that the clause
bounded **monotonicity, two endpoints and five sampled points — not the shape**:

> `v + (s + 0.05*sin(4*pi*s))*(vFloor - v)`, published self-consistently in
> `constants.skill.blend`, `blendSpelling()` and `blendValue()` together, is monotone, exact at
> every setting the clause sampled, and shipped **60/60 gates, 632 tests, 2/2 variants current** —
> while moving the resolved pool by up to **2.3 VPIP points** at settings the 0.01 slider can select
> (pool 50.57 at s = 0.10 where linear gives 52.00).

The shipped flag says *"the path between them is linear"*, and that sentence was true of the code
and unbounded by the gate. **I37(b) now asserts the shape**: linear in `s` is a **second difference
of zero**, walked at the page's own slider step with a 1e-9-point IEEE guard — seven orders of
magnitude below the 0.15-point move one slider notch makes at the tightest probe. The refuters'
own ripple is red at the first interior setting (second difference 0.0064 against a guard of 1e-9),
and `test/skill.test.mjs` carries the ripple as an armed control so the check cannot go blind.

The endpoint assertions were also moved to **literal** `s = 0` and `s = 1` on a refuter's
recommendation: a constant cannot be asked to certify itself, and reading them through `K.ref` /
`K.max` moved the assertion with the constant.

### 3. The badge map had no gated consumer

One refuter left `UNANCHORED` intact and deleted the **branch in `constHTML` that reads it**. The
badge left the Method view with the skill family and 47 tests still green — the P1 failure (a flag
deleted with everything green) displaced one level. I38(f) now asserts the reader as well as the
table, the way `gates/baseline.mjs` already does for `baselineQuant`.

### 4. `evCut.seUnit` — the anchor is true, and nothing enforced it

This is the P2 red team's third outcome, which §6 does not enumerate: *the anchor is true and
enforced by nothing.* All three refuters confirmed the numbers reproduce exactly — `sePt` is
`50/√100,000 = 0.15811388300841894`, spike S-B's 0.1581 — and all three reported the same hole:

* `sePt: 0.9`, `seBBMean: 0.5` and `trials: 12345`, **typed in place of their derivations**, ship
  60/60 gates, the whole suite and both variants current;
* the reason is structural: `verify.mjs`'s CLI calls `stampConstants` **before** `verifyModel`, so
  I40(d)'s `Object.is(shipped.seUnit[f], fresh.seUnit[f])` compares the derivation with itself;
* `test/ev-cut.test.mjs` — the one check that reads the model off disk unstamped — did not carry
  `seUnit` in its field loop;
* nothing downstream consumes it: the band reads each cell's own `seBB`.

**No number was invented and none was flagged**, because badging a derived identity `estimate`
would be a lie on the page. What ships instead is the assertion the prose already implied, in both
places:

* **I40(d)** now asserts `seUnit.trials === model.meta.trials.cell` and
  `Object.is(seUnit.sePt, seOfTrials(that count))` — against the **model and the accessor**, not
  against `fresh` — and `seBBMean` against a **second walk of the same 21 seats written in the
  gate**, independently of `ev-band.mjs`.
* **`test/ev-cut.test.mjs`** carries the same three identities against the on-disk model, plus
  `sePt === 50/√trials` and an armed control that the two shipped trial counts differ (so sourcing
  `sePt` from the wrong one is detectable).

Each was armed by perturbing `ev-band.mjs` itself and re-stamping, which is what the refuters did:
all three typed derivations are now red.

### 5. Recorded, not repaired

These are findings the phase accepted as accurate and did not act on. They are here so that the next
phase inherits them as knowledge rather than as a surprise.

* **`stampConstants` runs before the gates**, so *every* field-by-field `Object.is` comparison in
  I40(d) is a self-comparison under the house GREEN command — including `mixK`, `t4Mass` and
  `derivedAt.state`. What actually bounds the block is the **bracket** (`evMassAtK ≤ t4Mass ≤
  evMassNextStep`, which every wrong derivation the refuters built failed *after* a full restamp and
  rebuild), the new identity assertions, and the unit test reading the file unstamped. The gate's
  own detail line now says this rather than crediting itself with a comparison it cannot make.
* **`derivedAt.state` cannot catch a fingerprint that stops fingerprinting.** Gutting `evDefaultKey`
  to `return 'default'` is green, because both the gate and the test compare the field against that
  same function. `pairs` and `t4Band` are checked against live quantities and do bite.
* **Prose is bounded by length and substrings, never by meaning.** `constants.skill.flag` accepts a
  61-character *denial* ("both perfectly fine and fully anchored") and `evCut.derivation` accepts
  the two words it greps for. Both are the standing limitation of the I41(g)/I44(f) idiom; the
  cheap repair the memos propose — requiring a phrase from §6's own row — is left for a phase that
  can weigh it against the same treatment for every other flag.
* **The detent set is asserted in one direction only.** Every shipped detent must land on a measured
  lattice row; nothing requires every landing setting to be shipped, so `[0, 1]` and `[]` are green.
  The forcing argument is arithmetic (`55 − 30s ∈ {25, 40, 55} ⟺ s ∈ {0, 0.5, 1}`) and is now
  additionally protected by I38(g), which pins the domain the enumeration runs over.
* **`constants.skill.detents` still has no consumer on the page** — `#vpskill` carries no ticks —
  which is the item-9 forward-declaration posture, and the shell's comment overstates it.
* **`BUD.total` is unpinned**: raising it alone hands `core` headroom no test objects to, because
  `CORE_BUDGET` is derived from it. `test/gates-variants.test.mjs`'s regexes pin the two *core*
  readings (`/120K`, `/13K`), which is what caught the naive raise; pinning the `BUD` object the way
  `test/variant.test.mjs` pins the variant budgets is the one-line close.
* **The plays-better reach bound is lexical where it scans and behavioural where it probes.** One
  refuter wired an invented `postflopEdge` coefficient, read off the state, and shipped it green;
  two others showed that any *skill-keyed* pathway is caught, because the dial resolves into a VPIP
  before `solve` runs and every v-driven effect is swept by the frozen legacy fixtures. Both are
  true: I38(e) bounds the *shipped* coefficient at reach zero and says nothing about a second
  coefficient built under another name — which is I33(g)'s memo-scope problem, not this row's.
* **One provenance correction accepted:** the `modelCode` row's measurement is quoted as 53,353 B
  where the live reading is 53,418 B (52.17K, +3.51% under the 54K ceiling rather than +3.6%). The
  ceiling is not moved; the arithmetic is restated in V3-PLAN §6.
